# Coffee and Health
Coffee is one of the most widely consumed beverages in the world and contains caffeine, antioxidants, and other bioactive compounds.
Moderate coffee consumption, typically 3 to 4 cups per day, is associated in observational studies with a lower risk of type 2 diabetes, Parkinson's disease, and liver disease.
Coffee can increase alertness and concentration, and may improve physical performance in the short term.
Excessive coffee can cause anxiety, insomnia, increased heart rate, and digestive discomfort. People sensitive to caffeine, pregnant people, and people with certain heart conditions should limit intake.
Coffee itself is low in calories, but added sugar, cream, and syrups can substantially increase calories.
